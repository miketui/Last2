## Claims policy

Do not invent:

- Reviews
- Testimonials
- Awards
- Bestseller status
- Sales numbers
- Celebrity associations
- Guaranteed outcomes

Unverified claims must be tagged:

```text
[VERIFY]
```

## Visual rules

- Keep typography readable.
- Use motion as enhancement, not the content itself.
- Critical flows should be low-motion and accessible.
- Mobile-first layout required.
